# Showdown Arena

Showdown Arena is a browser-based arena fighting game built around chaotic 1v1 battles, unique characters, boss fights, unlock progression, and arcade-style side modes. Every fighter has their own stats, weapon behavior, abilities, cooldowns, and special mechanics, so matchups can feel completely different depending on who enters the arena.

The game runs entirely in the browser with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Features

- Fast arena combat with movement, jumping, knockback, projectiles, melee attacks, shields, traps, summons, and status effects
- Separate body-part health for head, torso, arms, and legs
- 16 playable characters with unique weapons, abilities, tutorials, descriptions, and backstories
- Gold, shards, rarity, character unlocks, duplicate conversions, and boss rewards
- Local human-vs-human play
- Human-vs-AI play with selectable AI difficulty
- Tournament mode with best-of-7 series rules
- Boss mode with 15 custom boss levels
- AI Duel Test mode with 10 simulated rooms and a live leaderboard
- Recreational modes including Speed Madness, Damage Mania, King of the Platform, Hunted Mode, Border Shrink, SWAP!, One vs All, and Draft Mode
- Multiple maps with hazards and terrain interactions
- Custom home screen artwork, sound effects, pause menu, volume controls, and progression UI

## Controls

Player 1:

- Move left: `A`
- Move right: `D`
- Jump: `W`
- Attack: `S`
- Primary ability: `Q`
- Secondary ability: `R`
- Climb/interact: `E`

Player 2:

- Move left: `Left Arrow`
- Move right: `Right Arrow`
- Jump: `Up Arrow`
- Attack: `Down Arrow`
- Primary ability: `/`
- Secondary ability: `.`
- Climb/interact: `'`

## Game Modes

### Play vs AI

Choose your character, select the AI difficulty, and fight a randomly selected AI opponent.

### Play vs Human

Two players fight locally on the same keyboard.

### Tournament Mode

A best-of-7 series where the first player to 4 wins takes the tournament. AI tournaments can award shards based on difficulty and final score.

### Boss Mode

Build a squad and progress through a full boss path. Bosses include staged fights, special mechanics, rewards, and unique hazards.

### AI Duel Test

An admin testing mode where 10 AI battles run at the same time. The live leaderboard tracks character wins and win rates for balance testing.

### Recreational Modes

Extra no-reward modes that remix the rules, including speed changes, damage changes, shrinking borders, draft choices, weapon and ability swapping, and one-vs-all challenges.

## Progression

Players earn gold and shards by completing matches, tournaments, tutorials, and boss levels. Shards unlock characters by rarity, while duplicates convert into gold. Some extra modes also require gold or unlock conditions before they can be played.

## About

Showdown Arena started as a small arena-combat idea and grew into a full browser game with character progression, boss campaigns, AI testing, and many experimental game modes. The main design goal is simple: every fighter should feel different, every mode should change the way the fight plays, and every match should have at least one ridiculous moment worth remembering.
